## Diligence

Diligence is Part of the **outer loop** (Delegation and Diligence) guides bigger decisions about when and how to use AI.

> taking repsonsibility for what you build, and making sure your AI use is transparent. ethical and accountable
>
> - Transparancy about ai use
> - Taking ownership of AI ouputs
> - verify work before shipping
> - cosindering the impact of what you built on others
> - honering privancy and other standards

> Three Habits of Diligence
>
> - Verification, seek out edge cases & assumptions
>   - Ask AI directly what assumptions it did
> - Tell people AI wrote it
> - Understanding
> - Consciously understand how the code works
