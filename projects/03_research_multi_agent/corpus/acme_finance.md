# Acme finance snapshot Q3 2026 (fictional)

Summary: Revenue, margins and investment capacity of Acme's heat pump business.

Acme's heat pump business had revenue of EUR 210 million in the last twelve months, 85% of it from Germany and Austria. Gross margin is 29%. The board has approved up to EUR 15 million for international expansion in 2027. Building a service network in a new country costs an estimated EUR 3-5 million in the first two years.
