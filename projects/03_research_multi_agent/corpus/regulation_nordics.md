# Regulation and subsidies (fictional)

Summary: Certification rules and subsidy schemes relevant for selling heat pumps in the Nordics.

All four countries accept EU CE marking and the EU Ecodesign rules. Sweden additionally requires installers to hold a national refrigerant certificate. Norway's Enova subsidy pays up to NOK 25,000 per installation, but only for units rated to at least -25 °C. Denmark offers a scrapping bonus for replacing oil boilers. Finland currently has no national subsidy for air-to-water heat pumps.
