# Customer feedback from the Swedish pilot (fictional)

Summary: Results of Acme's 2025 pilot with 120 households in southern Sweden.

Acme ran a pilot with 120 households in Skåne, southern Sweden, during winter 2025. Customer satisfaction was 4.3 out of 5. The most common complaint (38% of households) was that the controller app is not available in Swedish. During a cold spell in January, 22% of households saw the electric backup heater run for more than three days. Installers praised the compact outdoor unit.
