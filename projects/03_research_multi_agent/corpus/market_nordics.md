# Nordic heat pump market 2026 (fictional)

Summary: Market size and growth of residential heat pumps in Sweden, Norway, Finland and Denmark.

The four Nordic countries installed about 410,000 residential heat pumps in 2025. Sweden is the largest single market with roughly 160,000 units per year. Norway grows fastest, at 12% per year, driven by electricity-price subsidies. Finland's market shrank by 4% in 2025 after a subsidy programme ended. Air-to-water units make up 58% of new installations; ground-source units remain popular in rural Sweden. Analysts expect the Nordic market to reach 470,000 units per year by 2028.
