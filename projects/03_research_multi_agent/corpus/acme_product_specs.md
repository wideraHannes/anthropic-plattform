# Acme HeatFlow product line (fictional)

Summary: Technical specs of Acme's air-to-water heat pumps and their cold-climate limits.

Acme sells two air-to-water models: HeatFlow 8 (8 kW) and HeatFlow 12 (12 kW). Both are certified down to -15 °C outdoor temperature. Below -15 °C the units switch to a built-in electric backup heater, which roughly doubles running costs. A cold-climate variant rated to -28 °C is in development and planned for Q3 2027. HeatFlow units use R290 refrigerant, which already meets the EU F-gas phase-down rules. The controller app is available in German, English and French only.
