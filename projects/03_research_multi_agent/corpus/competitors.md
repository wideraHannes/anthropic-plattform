# Competitor landscape in the Nordics (fictional)

Summary: Market shares and strengths of the main heat pump brands in the Nordics.

NordVärme holds about 31% of the Nordic market and is strongest in Sweden. Fjell Energy has 18% share and leads in Norway with units certified to -30 °C. Three Asian brands together hold 27%, competing mainly on price, about 20% cheaper than European brands. Installers report that brand choice is driven mostly by cold-climate performance and by the availability of local service technicians.
